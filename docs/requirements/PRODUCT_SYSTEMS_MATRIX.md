# Product Systems Matrix

| System | Player can actively do | World can do independently | Must affect |
|---|---|---|---|
| Education | choose schools, applications, study/activity priorities, move, finance | school quality, admissions, coaches/teachers, peers evolve | career, skills, network, debt, geography, reputation |
| Career | apply, interview, negotiate, work, quit, pivot | openings, layoffs, managers, coworkers, firms change | income, skill, network, stress, location |
| Business | found, fund, advertise, hire, delegate, borrow, acquire, sell | customers, competitors, employees, investors react | cash, reputation, wealth, politics, family, legal |
| Property | buy, finance, rent, refinance, develop, sell | local market, tenants, vacancies, maintenance | cash flow, debt, wealth, geography, business |
| Markets | buy/sell, allocate, use leverage if enabled | generated firms/economy move prices | wealth, taxes, liquidity, legal exposure |
| Family | date, marry, parent, gift, support, separate | NPCs pursue goals, resent, reconcile, inherit | time, money, succession, reputation, network |
| Politics | run, fundraise, campaign, govern, appoint, negotiate | voters, parties, opponents, institutions react | law/tax environment, reputation, business, world |
| Crime/legal | choose abstract misconduct or lawful paths, hire counsel, contest | evidence, witnesses, enforcement, media evolve | freedom, money, reputation, family, office/business |
| Organizations | join, lead, fund, appoint, negotiate, seize/lose control | factions and leadership pursue goals | power, network, resources, world state |
| Dynasty | estate/succession choices, continue heir | heirs/siblings/rivals pursue their own goals | ownership, family history, future opportunities |
