# Content Authoring Guide

## Every event must answer
- Why is this event eligible now?
- Who is involved and what do they want?
- What engine facts created the situation?
- Does it interrupt at each time speed?
- What common choices exist?
- What action families are enabled for `Other...`?
- What are the deterministic consequence hooks?
- Which memories, reputations, evidence, ownership, or public-history facts can change?
- What does autonomy do if delegated?
- How is it summarized after fast-forward?

## Writing rules
- Concise, specific, human; normally 1-3 sentences before choices.
- Neutral tone. Do not moralize unconventional, wealthy, poor, criminal, political, or family paths; show consequences.
- Use generated/fictional schools, companies, countries, parties, politicians, and media by default.
- Sensitive illegal/violent actions are strategic abstractions. Never write real instructions for concealment, weapons, tactics, evasion, or harmful execution.
- Procedural text may vary wording, but structured facts and state changes remain deterministic.
