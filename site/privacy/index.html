Effective August 19, 2026

Privacy

Project Legacy does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data.

On-device gameplay

Your simulated world, choices, settings, recovery checkpoints, and journal are stored locally on your device. Project Legacy does not provide an account or developer-operated cloud save.

On-device intelligence

If you use the optional Other… field, the text and a bounded fictional game context are interpreted on the device. Supported devices may use Apple Foundation Models. Other devices use the bundled deterministic interpreter. Project Legacy does not send this text to a hosted model or to the developer.

Exports

Save export happens only when you request it. The app creates a checksummed .legacy package and opens the iOS share sheet. The destination you choose is governed by that destination’s privacy policy. Import reads only the file you select.

Diagnostics and tracking

The app contains no advertising SDK, analytics SDK, cross-app tracking, telemetry endpoint, or remote configuration dependency. Diagnostic facts used for recovery remain local.

Purchases

Version 0.1.0 does not enable in-app purchases. If purchases are introduced later, this policy and the App Store privacy disclosure will be updated before release.

Children

Project Legacy is not directed to children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

Material changes will be posted here with a new effective date. For privacy or support questions, use the contact method on the support page.
